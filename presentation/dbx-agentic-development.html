The system map  ·  what a Payment Service Provider is

A store never talks to Visa.
The PSP does.

Every card payment rides one chain: the shopper, the store, a Payment Service Provider, and the card networks. The store never talks to Visa — the PSP does. Stripe, Adyen, PagSeguro, and Cielo all play this role: take the shopper's money, keep a small fee, and settle with the store a few days later. That delay is where the PSP's own money is at risk.

The loss lands on the PSP
The PSP has usually already paid the store. A careless or fraudulent store leaves the PSP absorbing the chargeback — which is exactly the number we will score in this workshop.

Why this matters · three truths

Agents are real production labor

Claude Code and Codex edit repos every day. The risk is not that they write code — it's that nobody can prove what they changed and why.

  • plan-before-edit, not autopilot
  • a contract names the boundary
  • evidence is captured per change
Truth 2
Databricks collapses the stack

Ingest, quality, deploy, govern, and investigate live in one serverless platform. No separate orchestrator, no DIY lineage, no hand-rolled data checks.

  • Lakeflow for the pipeline
  • DQX for quality, UC for lineage
  • DABs for deploy, Genie for answers
Truth 3
Quality is preserved, not dropped

Real payment systems see null IDs, bad currencies, orphaned orders. Dropping them hides the story. Quarantining them makes it explainable.

  • _errors / _warnings columns
  • seven quarantine causes, all queryable
  • one held valid chargeback to replay

The meaning
The late chargeback is valid data — it belongs in Silver and Gold. The temporal dispute is invalid — it belongs in quarantine. Quality and risk are two different axes.
Never say
“m-007 committed fraud.” Say: m-007 has 1,209 quarantined transactions and one chargeback.
